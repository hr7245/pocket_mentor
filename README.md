# D2C Pocket Mentor (0-to-1 Brain)

A stage-gated **Pocket Mentor & Knowledge Center** built for **bootstrapped, first-time D2C founders with no mentor**.

## Core Philosophy
- **Cash is oxygen:** Every recommendation optimizes for runway, low MOQ, and positive Contribution Margin (CM1/CM2).
- **Stage-gated:** Founders don't see "Meta Ads" or "Investor Pitch Decks" on Day 1—they see what matters *right now* so they don't burn cash out of order.
- **Actionable over theoretical:** Checklists, "What NOT to do" guardrails, calculators, and templates.

## The 0-to-1 Journey (Knowledge Brain Structure)
1. **Stage 0: Foundation & Guardrails** — What NOT to do from Day 1, Co-founder vs. Solo, Incorporation & Compliance basics, Budgeting & Prioritizing.
2. **Stage 1: Product & Sourcing** — Validating before ordering, Sourcing manufacturers & negotiating low MOQs, Unit Economics (COGS, Shipping, RTO, CM1/CM2), What to do *while waiting* for samples/inventory.
3. **Stage 2: Launch & First 100 Orders (Zero-Budget)** — Storefront MVP, Logistics & 3PL basics, Organic & community acquisition, Nano-influencer seeding, WhatsApp/Email retention.
4. **Stage 3: Scaling & Preparing for Capital (Optional)** — When (and when NOT) to run paid ads, Hiring your first team/freelancers, Obstacles & troubleshooting, Building a pitch deck & preparing for investors.

## Directory Structure
- `knowledge-brain/` — Structured markdown playbooks, mentor insights, checklists, and calculators that power the Pocket Mentor's AI/app brain.
