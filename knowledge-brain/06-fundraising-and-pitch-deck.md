# Stage 3.5: Fundraising, Investor Prep & Pitch Deck

## 1. Should You Even Raise Money?
* **Don't raise** just to pay yourself a salary or figure out product-market fit. Investors fund *traction*, not ideas.
* **Raise only when:** You have proven unit economics (positive CM1/CM2), repeat customers, and you need capital specifically to **scale working capital (inventory)** or **accelerate proven ad channels**.

---

## 2. Preparing for Investments (What Investors Check)
Before pitching any angel or D2C micro-VC, have these 5 numbers ready in a clean 1-page tracker:
1. **Monthly Revenue & Growth Rate** (last 3–6 months)
2. **Gross Margin % & CM1/CM2 %** (unit economics)
3. **Blended CAC vs. LTV** (and CAC Payback Period)
4. **Repeat Purchase Rate** (30/60/90-day)
5. **Inventory Turnover / Cash Conversion Cycle**

---

## 3. Finding the Right Investors
* **Best early-stage fit for D2C:**
  * **Operator Angels:** Founders of scaled D2C brands (they bring mentorship + distribution, not just cash).
  * **D2C-focused Micro-VCs & Syndicates:** Funds that write \$25k–\$250k checks specifically for consumer brands.
  * **Revenue-Based Financing (RBF):** Non-dilutive capital for inventory/ads once you have consistent monthly revenue (e.g., Klub, Velocity, Wayflyer, Clearco).
* **Warm Intros > Cold Emails:** Engage with operator angels on LinkedIn/Twitter with smart questions, or ask founders you've helped for an intro.

---

## 4. The 10-Slide Bootstrapped-to-Seed D2C Pitch Deck
Keep it to **10–12 slides max**, visual, and numbers-first:
1. **Title & Hook:** Brand name, 1-line positioning, and hero product photo.
2. **The Problem:** What specific consumer pain point is broken today?
3. **The Solution / Product:** Your hero SKUs, why they're 10x better, and customer love/reviews.
4. **Why Now / Market:** Target audience size (be realistic, bottom-up math).
5. **Traction (The Proof):** Revenue growth, organic/seeded orders, repeat rate.
6. **Unit Economics:** Selling price, COGS, Gross Margin, CM1/CM2, CAC, LTV.
7. **Go-To-Market & Growth:** How you got your first 100–1,000 customers cheaply, and how you'll scale.
8. **Competitive Landscape:** A 2x2 matrix showing your clear wedge (e.g., Price vs. Quality/Clean Ingredients).
9. **Team:** Why *you* (domain insight, hustle, complementary skills).
10. **The Ask & Use of Funds:** How much you're raising, runway it buys, and exact milestones (e.g., *"50% Inventory, 30% Proven Ads, 20% Key Hire"*).
