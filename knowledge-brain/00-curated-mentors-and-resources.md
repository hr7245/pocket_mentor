# Curated D2C & Bootstrapped Mentors & Resources

A curated knowledge base of operators, podcasts, playbooks, and newsletters for bootstrapped, first-time D2C founders.

## 1. Top Podcasts (Real Operator Playbooks)
- **Founder Thesis (Akshay Datt)** — Deep-dive interviews with profitable/bootstrapped consumer brand founders breaking down product-channel fit and unit economics.
- **The Bootstrapped Founder (Arvid Kahl)** — Mental models for solo/lean operators, building in public, cash flow management, and organic growth.
- **Startups For the Rest of Us (Rob Walling & Mike Taber)** — Gold standard for sustainable, non-VC growth, pricing psychology, and escaping the VC treadmill.
- **Ecommerce Conversations (Practical Ecommerce)** — Physical product businesses, inventory management, and multi-channel D2C scaling.
- **GoKwik Podcast / Ecosystem Playbooks** — Mechanics of e-commerce conversion, Cash on Delivery (COD) management, RTO reduction, and logistics.

## 2. Playbooks & Tactical Guides
- **The D2C Scaling Playbook** — Unit economics (gross margins after commissions, gateways, taxes), logistics/RTO negotiation, and 90-day repeat purchase benchmarks (aiming for 30%+).
- **Indie Hackers Case Studies** — Transparent post-mortems and revenue breakdowns from bootstrapped creators.
- **Headstart Video Hub & D2C Blaze Masterclasses** — Tactical panels on omnichannel and early-stage execution.

## 3. Newsletters & Blogs
- **Trends.vc** — Concise reports on niche e-commerce trends, micro-communities, and consumer behavior.
- **StartupFox (Sabyasachi Chandra)** — Analytical breakdowns on consumer brands and retail unit economics.
- **First 1000** — How iconic consumer and tech startups found their very first 1,000 customers.

## 4. Non-Negotiable Mentor Rules for Bootstrapped D2C
1. **Contribution Margin > Gross Revenue:** Track CM2 (after performance marketing & logistics) per SKU—never chase vanity GMV.
2. **Obsess Over Retention Early:** Paid ads get more expensive every year; aim for a 30%+ 90-day repeat purchase rate.
3. **De-risk Inventory:** Cash trapped in slow-moving inventory kills more bootstrapped D2C brands than competition. Negotiate small-batch MOQs.
4. **Target 55–65%+ Gross Margin:** Anything lower leaves no room for shipping, returns/RTO, payment fees, and marketing.
