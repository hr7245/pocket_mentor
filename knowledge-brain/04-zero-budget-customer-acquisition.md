# Stage 2.5: Zero-Budget & High-ROI Customer Acquisition

## 1. The Cheapest, Highest-ROI Ideas (First 100–500 Orders)
1. **Nano-Influencer Product Seeding (Cost: Product + Shipping):**
   * Find 50–100 creators (1k–15k followers) who *already* post about your niche.
   * Send them a free product with a personalized handwritten note—no strict script, just ask for honest feedback/a story.
   * **Bonus:** Ask permission to repost their content as UGC (User-Generated Content) on your page and future ads.
2. **Founder-Led "Build in Public" Content:**
   * Short-form video (Reels/TikTok/Shorts): *"Day 12 of building my skincare brand from my bedroom,"* *"Why I rejected 4 suppliers,"* *"Packing my first 10 orders."* People buy from founders they root for.
3. **Niche Communities (Zero Ad Spend):**
   * Reddit, Facebook Groups, Discord, WhatsApp communities. Don't spam links—answer questions genuinely, share your learnings, and let curious people click your profile.
4. **Post-Purchase Referral & Review Loop:**
   * Inside every package, include a card: *"Share a photo/story tagging @yourbrand and get 15% off your next order (or a free mini)."*
5. **Local Pop-Ups & Offline Collabs:**
   * Partner with complementary local cafes, gyms, salons, or weekend markets to get face-to-face feedback and instant cash sales.

---

## 2. Retention: The Secret to Surviving Tight Budgets
Acquiring a new customer is 5x–7x more expensive than keeping an existing one.
* **Target:** **30%+ 90-day repeat purchase rate** (for consumables) or strong referral rate (for durables).
* **3 Automated Flows to Set Up on Day 1 (Email + WhatsApp):**
  1. **Abandoned Cart (1 hr & 24 hr):** Remind them + answer top objection (shipping/returns).
  2. **Post-Purchase Care:** *"Your order is on the way! Here's how to get the best results from it."*
  3. **Replenishment / Cross-Sell (Day 25–45):** *"Running low? Reorder with 1 click."*

---

## 3. When (and How) to Start Paid Ads
* **Only start paid ads when:**
  1. You have **50–100+ organic/seeded sales** and proven repeat/positive reviews.
  2. Your **CM1 is >45%** and you know your break-even ROAS.
* **How to test cheaply:**
  * Start with a small daily budget you can afford to lose for 14 days.
  * Test **3 distinct creative angles** (Problem/Solution, Founder Story, UGC/Unboxing)—creative *is* your targeting.
  * Kill losers after 3–5 days; double down only on creatives with profitable ROAS.
