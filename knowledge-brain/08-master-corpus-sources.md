# Master Corpus Sources & Extracted 0-to-1 Insights

## 1. Core Actionable Insights Extracted from the Corpus
- **Do Things That Don't Scale (Paul Graham / YC):** Manually recruit your first 10–50 customers, hand-deliver or write personal notes, and talk to every early buyer. Don't automate or buy ads before you understand why people buy.
- **Default Alive vs. Default Dead (Paul Graham):** Know your monthly burn and growth rate. For bootstrapped D2C, this means **positive CM1/CM2 per order** and a **<60-day CAC payback period**.
- **The Mom Test (Rob Fitzpatrick):** Never ask "Would you buy my product?" Ask about their *past behavior* ("What did you buy last month? What annoyed you about it?").
- **Growth Loops > Funnels (Reforge / Andrew Chen):** Build self-reinforcing loops (e.g., Unboxing → Instagram Story Tag → 15% Off Next Order → New Customer) instead of relying solely on linear paid ad funnels.
- **The Dangerous Seduction of LTV (Bill Gurley):** Never use inflated 3-year LTV projections to justify high Day-1 CAC. Optimize for **first-order or 60-day payback**.
- **RTO & Checkout Optimization (GoKwik / India D2C):** In high-COD markets, instant WhatsApp/IVR confirmation + prepaid incentives (free shipping / small discount) cut RTO from 25–35% down to 10–15%.
- **16 Startup Metrics (a16z) & Pricing 101 (Sequoia/YC):** Price for value and margin (55–65%+ Gross Margin), not cost-plus 2x.
