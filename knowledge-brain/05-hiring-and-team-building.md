# Stage 3: Hiring & Team Building on a Tight Budget

## 1. The Bootstrapped Hiring Rule
**Do NOT hire full-time employees until the founder is drowning in a *proven, repeatable* task.**
* **Phase 1 (0–100 orders/mo):** Founder does everything (customer support, packing, content, supplier follow-ups).
* **Phase 2 (100–500 orders/mo):** Hire **part-time freelancers / interns** for specific bottlenecks (e.g., video editing, graphic design, packing help).
* **Phase 3 (500+ orders/mo):** Make your first full-time hire.

---

## 2. The First 3 Hires for a Lean D2C Brand
1. **Operations / Fulfillment & Customer Support Associate:** Frees the founder from daily packing, courier tracking, and "Where is my order?" tickets.
2. **Content Creator / Video Editor (Freelance/Part-time):** Turns raw founder clips and UGC into daily Reels/TikToks/Ad creatives.
3. **Performance Marketer (Only when scaling ads):** Never hire a junior to "figure out ads" from scratch—hire someone part-time/fractional who has already scaled a similar AOV (Average Order Value) brand.

---

## 3. How to Hire Safely Without Burning Cash
* **Paid Test Projects:** Always give a small, paid 3–5 day test task before any commitment.
* **SOPs First:** Before delegating, record a 3-minute Loom video of yourself doing the task and write a 5-step checklist.
* **Incentive Alignment:** For growth/sales roles, combine a modest base with performance-linked bonuses.
