# Stage 1: Sourcing, Unit Economics & Waiting-Period Playbook

## 1. Sourcing Manufacturers & Negotiating Low MOQs
* **Where to find them:** Local manufacturing clusters (e.g., Tier-2/Tier-3 hubs), B2B directories (IndiaMART, Alibaba, Thomasnet, Maker's Row), and trade expos.
* **White-label vs. Custom:** Start with **white-label / private-label** (existing proven formula/design with your branding) or minor tweaks. Custom R&D takes 6–12 months and burns cash.
* **How to negotiate low MOQs (200–500 units):**
  1. Offer to pay **100% of raw material cost upfront** and labor upon dispatch.
  2. Position yourself as a **long-term growth partner** ("We're doing a 200-unit test batch; if it sells out in 30 days, we'll reorder 1,000").
  3. **Never** skip ordering physical samples from at least 3 suppliers before committing.

## 2. Unit Economics (The Survival Math)
Never price a product as `2x Manufacturing Cost`. Use this **Contribution Margin Waterfall**:

* **Target Gross Margin:** **55% to 65%+**
  $$\text{Gross Margin} = \frac{\text{Selling Price (Excl. Tax)} - \text{COGS}}{\text{Selling Price (Excl. Tax)}}$$
* **Example Per-Unit Waterfall (Target):**
  * Net Selling Price (after discounts, excl. tax): `100%`
  * COGS (Product + Packaging): `-30% to -35%`
  * Shipping + Reverse Shipping (weighted for returns/RTO): `-10% to -15%`
  * Payment Gateway / COD Fee: `-2% to -3%`
  * **Contribution Margin 1 (CM1):** **`47% to 58%`** (This is what's left to pay for marketing and overhead).

**True CAC Formula (accounting for returns/RTO):**
$$\text{True CAC} = \frac{\text{Ad Spend per Order}}{1 - \text{Return/RTO Rate}}$$

## 3. What to Do While Waiting for Products/Samples (Zero Wasted Days)
Manufacturers take 2–8 weeks. **Do NOT sit idle.** Use this exact checklist while waiting:

1. **Build Your Waitlist / Pre-Launch Audience:**
   * Put up a 1-page "Coming Soon" landing page with a clear problem/solution hook and email/WhatsApp capture ("Get 20% off + early access").
2. **Create Your Content Bank:**
   * Film "Building in Public" behind-the-scenes videos (why you started, sample testing, packaging unboxing).
   * Write 10–15 educational/problem-solving posts for Instagram/TikTok/LinkedIn/Reddit.
3. **Line Up 50 Nano-Influencers:**
   * Build a spreadsheet of 50 creators (1k–15k followers) in your niche. Warm up relationships by commenting on their posts so you can DM them the day your batch arrives.
4. **Set Up Storefront & Retention Flows:**
   * Configure Shopify/WooCommerce, payment gateways, shipping zones, and automated **Abandoned Cart** + **Welcome** email/WhatsApp flows.
5. **Interview 10–15 Target Customers:**
   * Ask them what they currently buy, what they hate about it, and what would make them switch. Use their exact words in your website copy.
