# Stage 2: Logistics, Operations & Common Obstacles

## 1. Logistics & Fulfillment (0-to-1 Setup)
* **First 100–300 orders:** Pack and ship from home. You will learn which packaging breaks, which couriers are slow, and what customers ask.
* **Use a Shipping Aggregator:** Instead of negotiating with individual couriers on Day 1, use aggregators (e.g., Shiprocket, Pickrr, Shippo, EasyPost) to compare rates and serviceability across pin/zip codes.
* **When to move to a 3PL (Third-Party Logistics):** Only when you are shipping **300+ orders/month** consistently and packing takes >2 hours/day of founder time.

---

## 2. Managing COD (Cash on Delivery) & RTO (Return to Origin)
In markets with high COD (like India, SEA, MENA), RTO (undelivered returns) can kill a bootstrapped brand:
1. **Automated Order Confirmation:** Send an instant WhatsApp/SMS/IVR confirmation for every COD order. If they don't confirm, **cancel before shipping**.
2. **Incentivize Prepaid:** Offer a small perk for prepaid orders (e.g., free shipping or ₹50 / \$2 off) instead of raising prices.
3. **Address Validation:** Flag incomplete addresses (missing landmark/house number) before dispatch.

## 3. Common Obstacles & How to Survive Them
| Obstacle | Why It Happens | Bootstrapped Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Supplier delays** | Raw material shortages, festival seasons, low priority for small orders | Always pad timelines by 2–3 weeks; never run ads before inventory is physically in hand. |
| **Stockouts on hero SKU** | Didn't track lead time vs. daily sales velocity | Reorder when you have `Lead Time (days) × Daily Sales + 14 days` of stock left. |
| **High customer returns** | Product doesn't match photos, sizing issues, or transit damage | Add real unedited photos/videos, clear sizing/usage guides, and drop-test your packaging. |
| **Cash crunch** | Cash locked in slow-moving SKUs or marketplace payouts (15–30 day delays) | Run a flash bundle/clearance on slow SKUs to convert boxes back into cash; prioritize direct website sales. |
| **Copycats** | Competitors copy your product/ads | Your moat is **brand story, community, customer service, and speed of iteration**—not secrecy. |
