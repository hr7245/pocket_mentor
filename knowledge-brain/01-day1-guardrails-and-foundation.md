# Stage 0: Day 1 Guardrails, Foundation & Budgeting

## 1. What NOT to Do from Day 1 (The Cash-Burn Traps)
1. **Do NOT rent an office or warehouse.** Work from home/garage until you outgrow it.
2. **Do NOT run Meta/Google ads on Day 1.** Ads amplify product-market fit; they do not create it.
3. **Do NOT order custom-printed boxes/jars with high MOQs.** Use plain stock packaging + a custom sticker or stamp for your first 200–500 orders.
4. **Do NOT hire an agency** (marketing, branding, or PR) before you have 100+ organic sales and know your customer's exact objections.
5. **Do NOT build a custom-coded website.** Use Shopify (or WooCommerce) with a clean, fast free/cheap theme.
6. **Do NOT launch with 10+ SKUs.** Launch with 1–3 hero SKUs. More SKUs = trapped cash in dead inventory.
7. **Do NOT obsess over logos, trademarks, or fancy visiting cards** before validating that strangers will pay for your product.

---

## 2. Co-Founder vs. Going Solo
* **When to go solo:** If you can handle either product/sourcing OR marketing/sales yourself and can hire freelancers for gaps. Solo = faster decisions, 100% equity, zero co-founder drama.
* **When to find a co-founder:** Only if they bring a **complementary, non-overlapping superpower** (e.g., you are strong in product/ops, they are strong in growth/content/sales).
* **How to test a co-founder (before giving equity):**
  * Work on a **30-day sprint** together (e.g., validating the product or getting the first 20 pre-orders) *before* incorporating or splitting equity.
  * **Always use a 4-year vesting schedule with a 1-year cliff**—even for best friends or family.

---

## 3. Incorporation & Compliance Basics
* **Start lean:** You can validate demand (landing page, waitlist, pre-orders, or small batch tests) before heavy incorporation.
* **When you start selling regularly:**
  * **Sole Proprietorship / LLC (or Pvt Ltd if planning to raise VC):** Pick the simplest, cheapest legal structure that lets you open a business bank account and payment gateway.
  * **Tax Registration:** Register for GST / Sales Tax / VAT as required in your region so you can legally invoice and claim input tax credits on manufacturing/ads.
  * **Category-Specific Licenses:**
    * *Food/Beverage/Supplements:* FSSAI / FDA / local food safety registration.
    * *Cosmetics/Skincare:* Cosmetic manufacturing/import license & ingredient labeling compliance.
    * *Electronics:* Safety certifications (BIS, CE, FCC).
  * **Basic IP:** Do a quick trademark/domain/social-handle check *before* printing labels so you don't get forced to rebrand later.

---

## 4. How to Budget & Prioritize (The Bootstrapped Rule)
Divide your starting capital into **3 strict buckets**:
1. **40% — Inventory & Packaging (Small Batch):** Enough for 100–300 units. Never put 80% of your cash into inventory.
2. **30% — Content, Seeding & Storefront:** Domain, Shopify basic plan, sample shipping to micro-influencers, basic product photography.
3. **30% — Emergency Buffer / Working Capital:** Shipping surprises, returns, restocking your winning SKU.

**Priority Filter:** Before spending ₹1 / \$1, ask: *"Does this directly help me get my first 100 paying customers or fulfill their orders?"* If no, defer it.
